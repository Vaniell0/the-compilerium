## What this is

«Learning representations by back-propagating errors», Nature 1986: Rumelhart, Hinton, Williams showed that backpropagation trains hidden layer representations in multilayer networks; the paper cited as «the invention of backprop» though the algorithm was known earlier (Werbos 1974, Linnainmaa 1970).

## Why it matters

HINTON-C001: the paper is empirical proof that backprop works in practice in neural networks, not a mathematical invention of the algorithm; that is precisely Hinton's role — he showed it works in neural networks, not re-derived the chain rule. Supports the distinction between «proved it works» and «invented the method».
