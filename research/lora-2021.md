## What this is

«LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models» (Hu et al., 2021): instead of fine-tuning all model weights — add a pair of low-rank matrices (A × B) to each weight matrix; only these matrices are trained; the original weights are frozen; at inference the matrices are folded back → zero overhead.

## Why it matters

DISTILLATION-C003: Lumina V6 uses 2K LoRA parameters on top of a 202K base model for feature distillation adaptation; LoRA here is not a personalization tool but a mechanism for fine-tuning on the V-JEPA teacher signal without full retraining of the whole network. Supports a future claim about parameter-efficient adaptation as a distinct competence from full fine-tuning.
