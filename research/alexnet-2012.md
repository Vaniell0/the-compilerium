## What this is

«ImageNet Classification with Deep Convolutional Neural Networks», NeurIPS 2012: Krizhevsky, Sutskever, Hinton won ImageNet with top-5 error 15.3% vs 26.2% for second place; used ReLU, dropout, data augmentation, GPU training; the paper launched the deep learning revolution.

## Why it matters

HINTON-C002: AlexNet is practical proof that deep learning works on large-scale vision; the margin 15.3% vs 26.2% is not an incremental improvement but a qualitative jump. SUTSKEVER-C001: Sutskever is co-author; the start of his career. LECUN-C001: LeNet-5 (1998) was architecturally correct; AlexNet did not invent CNN — it proved that LeCun's architecture lacked compute and data, not the idea; LeCun waited 14 years for confirmation.
