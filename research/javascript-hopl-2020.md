## What it says

The paper traces JavaScript from Brendan Eich's 10-day prototype at Netscape (working name: Mocha) through ES2019, covering the JScript fork, the ECMA standardisation, the ES3/ES5 cleanup, and the ES2015 redesign. Eich co-authored it; the source material includes internal Netscape documents and the editor's notes from successive TC39 working groups.

## Why it matters here

JS-C001 (10 days, Mocha/LiveScript naming, "make it look like Java" brief) is not secondhand anecdote — it is documented by the creator in a peer-reviewed venue. JS-C002 (Sun/Oracle trademark, ECMAScript distinction) is also covered: the paper explains why the name "JavaScript" was chosen as a marketing move and what that decision cost later. JS-C008 (Scheme closures + Self prototypes + Java syntax) is stated explicitly by Eich; this is the canonical citation for the three-parent claim.
